Privacy & transparency

Written for students, not lawyers. The formal notice exists separately.

What we collect

At registration: your name, one contact channel and a password — nothing else. Your university then supplies roster details (student number, faculty, programme, campus) used to verify membership.

We do not collect date of birth, and we do not ask for a photo at registration.

Who can see what

The Guild sees aggregate activity only: reach, turnout, participation counts. The university sees counts and trends, never individual behaviour. Sponsors receive no personal data at all.

Poll privacy

CampusHub does not provide Guild or university users with a way to see how a named student responded to a poll. Polls are non-binding sentiment instruments.

The exact technical model behind this is under architecture review; this page will always describe what the system actually delivers, never more.

Student Voice identity

Your issue is pseudonymous to other students. Only explicitly authorised handlers can see your identity, each access is recorded, and you can see when it has been accessed.

University official access

Named university office-holders see counts, trends and category-level statistics only — no content, no identities, no individual records.

Sponsors

Sponsored content is labelled, limited, and shown only to broad audiences (for example, all verified students on a campus). Sponsors can never target you by behaviour, verification strength, or anything you have done.

Sponsored notifications are never sent.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, correct it, or request deletion at any time from Help & support. Deletion explains plainly what is removed, what is anonymised, and what must remain for security and accountability.
