    Privacy & transparency

    Plain language, before you register.

    What we collect

    Account contact (email/phone), display name, and — when you verify — the roster attributes your university supplies (student number, faculty, programme, campus, year). We do not collect date of birth for advertising.

    Who can see what

    • Other students: display name and campus only — no contact details, no poll answers, no Voice identity.
    • Guild administrators: aggregated counts and trends — not how a named student responded to a poll.
    • University officials: constrained official view — counts, category-level Voice statistics — no individual answers or Voice submitter identity.
    • Sponsors: never receive student personal data.

    Poll privacy

    CampusHub does not provide Guild or university users with a way to see how a named student responded to a poll. Results are shown only when privacy thresholds are met.

    Student Voice identity

    Your identity is visible only to authorised handlers and is not shared publicly. Every access is audited. Student Voice is not anonymous — it is accountable.

    University official access

    Limited to official notices where the licence grants it, plus the constrained analytics view. No individual poll answers, no Voice submitter identity, no member contact exports, no audit-log editing.

    Sponsors

    Prohibited categories (betting, predatory lending and others) never appear. No assurance-level or behavioural targeting. Sponsors never receive student personal data.

    Your rights

    You can request your data and request deletion. Deletion preserves auditable history where required and removes public availability. We cannot remotely delete files already downloaded to unmanaged devices.
