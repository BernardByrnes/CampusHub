How verification works

CampusHub verifies your membership using university-provided student records and approved institutional contact methods.

Each assurance step describes the evidence available for your membership. It does not make a universal claim about who can participate; each campus service sets its own access rules.

Typical access by assurance ›

These are typical defaults. Membership status, the campus service, its audience and its own requirements still apply.

  • L0: Read public tenant content only. No participation.
  • L1: Read appropriate campus information; save, follow, RSVP and Daily Quiz.
  • L2: Everything at L1, plus polls where the tenant permits L2 participation and Student Voice where enabled.
  • L3: Full student participation, including polls where the tenant sets a high-integrity threshold.
Why OTP provenance matters ›
A one-time code to a channel you provide proves you control that channel — not that you are that student. A channel supplied by the university or its roster may support stronger institutional proof where that method is configured. An institutional email supports L3 only where the university confirms that the account is bound to the student and reflects current enrolment, or has reliable status revocation. Otherwise it supports affiliation or contact only and is capped at L2. A domain suffix alone proves nothing.

    Privacy & transparency

    What CampusHub collects, who can see it and the choices you have.

    Last updated: 20 May 2026

    What we collect

    CampusHub Pilot collects information needed to run your active university membership and the features you choose to use.

    Account and profile information

    • Display name and the email or phone used for your account.

    Membership and verification information

    • Student number, faculty or college, programme, year, campus and residence where supplied/relevant.
    • Assurance / verification history, plus membership state.

    CampusHub activity

    • Saves, RSVPs, notifications, XP, Level and Streak.
    • Whether you participated in a poll, Student Voice submissions where you use Voice, and login or security history where applicable.

    Why: Account information runs the account and lets CampusHub contact you where needed. Membership and verification information puts you in the right university and campus context and supports eligibility decisions. Activity information makes the features you use work and keeps your own CampusHub history and state.

    CampusHub Pilot does not collect your date of birth.

    Who can see what

    Access depends on the role and active university membership involved.

    • Other students: do not get your contact details, individual poll answers or Student Voice identity through CampusHub. CampusHub does not provide a student directory.
    • Guild administrators: can access the membership and verification information needed to run CampusHub under their permissions. Authorised support lookups are limited and audited; they can show display name, assurance level, verification history, membership state and XP total. Contact details are not shown by default, and routine contact-detail bulk export is not available by default.
    • Guild administrators cannot see individual poll answers, Voice content authored or quiz answers through a support lookup.

    Poll privacy

    CampusHub does not provide Guild or university users with a way to see how a named student responded to a poll. Results are shown only when privacy thresholds are met.

    CampusHub knows that you participated in a poll so it can enforce one participation (one response), calculate turnout and award participation XP. That participation knowledge does not expose which answer you selected through CampusHub functionality.

    Student Voice identity

    Your identity is not shown publicly. Student Voice is pseudonymous to other students, but it is not anonymous to authorised handlers.

    Only an authorised Voice handler with separately granted identity access may reveal the submitter identity. That reveal requires a recorded reason, and every identity access is audited.

    University Officials do not receive Student Voice submitter identity.

    University official access

    University Officials have only the scoped access granted under the licence. Where licensed, they may publish official notices.

    They do not receive individual poll answers, Student Voice submitter identity, individual member behavioural records or member contact exports. They cannot edit the audit log.

    Where a constrained official analytics view is provided, it is limited to counts, trends and category-level Student Voice statistics.

    Sponsors

    Sponsors do not receive named individual student information through CampusHub. Sponsors do not receive student personal data.

    Sponsors cannot target by assurance, poll participation, Student Voice, quiz activity, browsing behaviour or other prohibited behavioural or personal dimensions.

    Betting and predatory-lending sponsorship is prohibited.

    Your rights

    Access

    You can request a copy of your personal data for this active university membership: your profile; membership and verification history; XP ledger and Streak; Saves, Follows and RSVPs; Student Voice issues you authored; notifications; and login history. It excludes another person’s data and individual ballots.

    Correction

    Information you supplied can be corrected through the appropriate profile flow. University or roster-derived fields cannot simply be overwritten by you; they require a correction request.

    Deletion

    A CampusHub membership deletion applies to the active university membership and may delete your profile, contact details, XP ledger, Saves, Follows, RSVPs, notifications and login history. Closing the global CampusHub user account is a separate action.

    A published Student Voice issue and its status history may remain as an anonymised institutional record after a membership deletion, subject to the final legal position. Aggregate counts may remain, and required audit entries remain for security and accountability.

    Public harmful content can be removed, restricted or redacted; the audit record of what happened remains.

    How to exercise these rights

    Use the data-rights contact in the Privacy Notice provided during registration to request access, correction or deletion. Pilot fulfilment may be assisted by staff rather than fully automated.

    Limits we are honest about

    We cannot remotely delete files already downloaded to unmanaged devices.

    CampusHub keeps information needed to operate the active membership and the features you use. Security and audit records required for accountability may remain after normal profile deletion. Published Voice history may remain anonymised as described above. CampusHub does not promise remote deletion of unmanaged downloaded copies.
